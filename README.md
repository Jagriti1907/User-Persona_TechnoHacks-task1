3. UX/Design Principles & Insights
​The following insights derived from the personas should guide all future design and feature development:
​A. Key User Motivations
​Career Advancement: (John, Sarah) - The product must clearly link skill acquisition to professional outcomes (jobs, promotions, resume boost).
​Validation: (Sarah) - Certification and verifiable achievements are critical for motivation and goal completion.
​Flexibility & Low Barrier: (Elara, John) - The learning experience must accommodate demanding schedules and avoid initial complexity to prevent drop-off.
